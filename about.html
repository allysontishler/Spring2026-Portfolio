I’m a UX researcher and designer with a background in human-centered design, serious games, and web development. I’m currently finishing my master’s in Media and Information, where I focus on UX and HCI.

My work sits at the intersections of research, interaction design, and play. I’m especially interested in how people learn, move through systems, and make sense of technology.

Having a cross-cultural background has shaped the way I think about design. It’s made me more aware of how design can influence inclusion, behavior, and the way people experience digital spaces.
