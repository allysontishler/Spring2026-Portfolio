Purrcel

RoleUX Researcher · UX Designer · UI Designer · Logo Designer
ContextTeam project (MI 839)

Overview

Purrcel is a narrative-driven 2D puzzle platformer exploring grief, memory, and emotional healing through calm, reflective gameplay. Players guide a spirit cat across a serene island, helping souls fulfill their final wishes.

My Role

Team Project

I led player research and UX design decisions, created personas and journey maps, and synthesized playtesting feedback into clear iteration priorities. I also designed and iterated the UI system (dialogue, menus, tips) and led logo iterations based on feedback.

Key Player Insights

  • Players need emotional clarity as much as mechanical clarity.
  • Subtle UI preserves immersion and supports reflection.
  • Clear feedback builds confidence and reduces hesitation.

Influencer matrix

The influencer matrix aligned the team on tone, pacing, UI density, and narrative feel across comparable games and media references.

UI designs

Dialogue uses a soft glow to reinforce the spirit-world vibe, while Asher’s grounded green palette mirrors his earth-connected character design.

The pause overlay clearly signals a state change while keeping the player visually connected to the scene.

A minimal settings layout prioritizes clarity and fast adjustments without breaking gameplay flow.

Audio controls use a sparkle-based slider that feels tactile and playful while matching the game’s magical tone.
